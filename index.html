GOOD TO KNOW

A few helpful answers.

How do I download my rent receipt?

Fill in the required details and select “Print / Save PDF”. For multiple months, confirm each selected payment first. Choose “Save as PDF” in the print window to save all prepared receipts as one file. The suggested filename uses the rent month or month range; your browser may let you change it.

Can I generate several months together?

Choose “Multiple months”, select a financial year and tick the months to include. Shared details are entered once. Each payment has its own amount, date, method, reference and receipt number. Confirm every payment before saving one combined PDF. Payment dates and confirmations are never filled automatically.

Can I record a UPI, bank or cash payment?

Yes. Choose how the rent was actually paid. For UPI, bank transfers or cheques, you can add a payment reference under “Additional details”. Keep the receipt with your payment record.

Do I need to choose a state?

It is optional for creating this general receipt. Choosing a state adds it to the property details and shows available legal references. It does not certify compliance or select a prescribed statutory form. State-specific research currently covers five jurisdictions; other locations still need review.

When should I include the landlord’s PAN?

For employer / HRA documentation, landlord PAN must be furnished when actual annual rent exceeds ₹1,00,000. In single mode, the annual estimate is rent × 12. In multi-month mode, it uses the average entered monthly payment × 12 (or the selected total for all 12 months). PAN switches on above the threshold; this is an estimate, not a calculation of your tax position. You can adjust the annual total, include PAN manually, or uncheck the option to omit it. Opting out changes the receipt, not your employer’s documentation requirements. The format check accepts recognised holder-type letters and a numeric sequence from 0001 to 9999. It does not verify issuance, the final check letter or ownership. HRA eligibility is not assessed by this tool.

Does this receipt need a signature or revenue stamp?

The template includes a recipient signature line. Check the relevant receipt and stamp rules for your situation. If a physical revenue stamp is needed, the “Signature & stamp” section lets you include space for one; a printed box is not a stamp or payment of duty.

How do I make next month’s receipt?

“Next month” keeps the people, property and amount, advances the period and receipt number, and clears the payment date and reference. Enter the actual payment details after receiving rent.

Where are my details saved?

They remain in this page’s memory. Refreshing or closing the page clears the details. Keep your downloaded PDF or printed copy.
