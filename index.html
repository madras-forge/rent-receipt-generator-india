    GOOD TO KNOW

    A few helpful answers.

    How do I download my rent receipt?

    Fill in the required details and select “Print / Save PDF”. In the print window, choose “Save as PDF” instead of a printer. Check the preview and save your copy.

    Can I record a UPI, bank or cash payment?

    Yes. Choose how the rent was actually paid. For UPI, bank transfers or cheques, you can add a payment reference under “Additional details”. Keep the receipt with your payment record.

    Do I need to choose a state?

    It is optional for creating this general receipt. Choosing a state adds it to the property details and shows available legal references. It does not certify compliance or select a prescribed statutory form. State-specific research currently covers five jurisdictions; other locations still need review.

    When should I include the landlord’s PAN?

    For employer / HRA documentation, landlord PAN must be furnished when actual annual rent exceeds ₹1,00,000. As you enter rent, the tool estimates a full year as rent × 12 and switches PAN on above that threshold. You can adjust the annual total, include PAN manually, or uncheck the option to omit it. Opting out changes the receipt, not your employer’s documentation requirements. The format check accepts recognised holder-type letters and a numeric sequence from 0001 to 9999. It does not verify issuance, the final check letter or ownership. HRA eligibility is not assessed by this tool.

    Does this receipt need a signature or revenue stamp?

    The template includes a recipient signature line. Check the relevant receipt and stamp rules for your situation. If a physical revenue stamp is needed, the “Signature & stamp” section lets you include space for one; a printed box is not a stamp or payment of duty.

    How do I make next month’s receipt?

    “Next month” keeps the people, property and amount, advances the period and receipt number, and clears the payment date and reference. Enter the actual payment details after receiving rent.

    Where are my details saved?

    They remain in this page’s memory. Refreshing or closing the page clears the details. Keep your downloaded PDF or printed copy.
